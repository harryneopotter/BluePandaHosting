

# QPANDA CLIENT AREA — COMPREHENSIVE COPY

---

## GLOBAL PRINCIPLES (for whoever implements this later)

* Nothing auto-changes without consent
* AI **suggests**, never acts silently
* Every screen should answer: *“Am I safe right now?”*
* Avoid words like: quantum, neural, teleport, autonomous
* Prefer words like: monitor, assist, recommend, review

---

## 1️⃣ LOGIN / ENTRY STATE

### Login Page Helper Text

> Access your hosting services, system status, and support — all in one place.

### Post-Login Welcome (first visit only)

> Welcome to your QPanda control area.
> From here, you can monitor your services, manage resources, and get help when you need it.
