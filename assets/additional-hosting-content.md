## General FAQ (Site-Wide)

### Account & Billing

**How do I get started?**  
Choose your plan, complete signup, and you'll receive login credentials within minutes. We can migrate your existing site for free or you can start fresh with a one-click WordPress install.

**What payment methods do you accept?**  
Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), PayPal, and bank transfer for annual plans.

**Can I cancel anytime?**  
Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime. We prorate remaining time for cancellations. 30-day money-back guarantee for new accounts.

**Do you offer refunds?**  
Full refund within 30 days, no questions asked. After 30 days, we prorate the remaining service period if you cancel.

**What happens when I upgrade?**  
Instant upgrade. You're charged the prorated difference for the current billing period, then the new price at renewal.

**Can I downgrade?**  
Yes, but downgrades take effect at your next renewal date to avoid service disruption.

**Do I need technical knowledge?**

Yes, domain registration is available as an add-on service. We also support domain transfers.

Included with all plans. Full IMAP/POP3/SMTP support, webmail, spam filtering, autoresponders, forwarders. Number of accounts varies by plan.

### Performance & Reliability

**What's your uptime guarantee?**  
99.9% for Entanglement and Superposition plans. 99.95% for Singularity plan. Actual network uptime typically exceeds 99.99%.

**How fast will my site load?**  
Depends on your site, but our infrastructure typically delivers:
- Static pages: 100-300ms response time
- WordPress with caching: 200-500ms
- E-commerce: 300-800ms
Much faster than budget hosting (often 1-3 seconds).

**What if my site gets a traffic spike?**  
CloudLinux resource allocation ensures you can handle your traffic without affecting others. LiteSpeed excels at concurrent connections. You won't go down when others would.

**Do you have backups?**  
Yes. Automated daily to hourly backups depending on plan. Retained 14-60 days. You can restore files, databases, or entire sites yourself through cPanel.

### Security Questions

**How secure is shared hosting?**  
Very secure with our stack:
- CloudLinux isolation (CageFS prevents account-to-account exploits)
- Imunify360 (AI-powered threat detection and blocking)
- ModSecurity (web application firewall)
- Daily malware scans with automatic cleanup
- DDoS protection at network level
- KernelCare (live security patching)

**What if my site gets hacked?**  
We scan daily and clean automatically when possible. If malware is detected, we notify you and assist with cleanup. JetBackup lets you restore to a pre-compromise state.

**Do you provide SSL certificates?**  
Yes, free SSL certificates on all plans via Let's Encrypt. Premium SSL options available for purchase.

**Is email secure?**  
Yes. SSL/TLS encryption for all connections. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC configured automatically. Spam and virus filtering included.

**Premium SSL (available for purchase):**

- $50-300/year depending on type

### Compliance Support

We can assist with:
- **PCI DSS** • For e-commerce sites accepting payments
- **GDPR** • Data protection and privacy requirements
- **HIPAA** • Healthcare data (requires dedicated server)
- **SOC 2** • Service organization controls (enterprise only)

Note: Compliance is a shared responsibility. We provide the infrastructure; you ensure your application complies with regulations.

### Backup Policies

**What we back up:**
- All files in your hosting account
- All databases
- Email accounts and messages
- cPanel configuration

**What we don't back up:**
- Content you've deleted more than 30-60 days ago (depending on plan)
- Sites removed from your account
- Backup frequency depends on your plan tier

**Your responsibility:**
While we provide automated backups, critical sites should also maintain off-server backups. We recommend:
- Periodic downloads of critical data
- Version control for code (Git)
- Database exports before major changes

## Getting Started Guide

**Step 1: Choose Your Plan**
- Start with Quantum Entanglement for single sites
- Choose Superposition if you have 2-5 sites or need more power
- Go with Singularity for maximum performance or many sites

**Step 2: Complete Signup**
- Select billing cycle (monthly or annual)
- Choose your domain (new, existing, or temporary)
- Complete payment

**Step 3: Access Your Account**
- Check email for cPanel login credentials
- Login to cPanel at yourdomain.com/cpanel
- Familiarize yourself with the interface

**Step 4: Install WordPress (or your CMS)**
- Use Softaculous one-click installer
- Choose your domain and directory
- Set admin username and password
- Install in 60 seconds

**Step 5: Configure Email**
- Create email accounts in cPanel
- Set up forwarding if needed
- Configure your email client (Outlook, Apple Mail, etc.)
- Or use webmail at yourdomain.com/webmail

**Step 6: Point Your Domain**
- Update nameservers at your domain registrar
- Or update A records if keeping nameservers elsewhere
- DNS propagation takes 1-24 hours

**Step 7: Migrate Existing Site (if applicable)**
- Request free migration assistance
- Or use cPanel's built-in migration tools
- Or upload files via FTP/File Manager

**Step 8: Test and Launch**
- Verify site loads correctly
- Test email delivery
- Check SSL certificate is active
- Review cPanel features you might need
